    01

    PixelDerm

    Lead ResearcherUI/UX Designer

    Tracking your skin’s health one pixel at a time.

    PixelDerm is an Android-based multi-algorithm framework designed to monitor and analyze UV-induced skin damage over time. It uses Adaptive Thresholding, Local Binary Patterns, and Connected Components Labeling in a structured pipeline to process images, extract texture features, and track spots. By transforming this analysis into clear risk scores, visual comparisons, and actionable advice, the application empowers individuals to actively manage their skin health.

    Signal no. 5

    UI/UX Designer

    Make the call. With Qwen3-4B running locally, the AI adapts the narrative to your household and decisions — even without cloud inference.

    A game built in under 24 hours for a Hackathon competition. Players create a household and face a storm countdown, pack a go-bag, secure their home, and check warnings. As the storm approaches, they respond to events and evacuation advisories, then see how their decisions affect the aftermath. A rule-based engine tracks each choice and gives players a preparedness score and personalized debrief, while a local AI model adds dialogue shaped by their household and decisions.

    Full-stack DeveloperUI/UX Designer

    A cozy little nook to plan your day, with a tomato friend who reacts to how you’re doing.

    A tomato-themed productivity web app that brings a to-do list, mood tracker, timers, and weather into one dashboard. Users build their own tomato avatar from layered, recolorable hand-drawn art, then keep it company by finishing tasks, logging moods to keep a streak alive, and running pomodoro sessions.
